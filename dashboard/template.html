Evaluation protocol

Preprocessing is fitted on the training set only. Categorical values use one-hot encoding; unseen values are ignored. Linear Regression also standardizes numeric inputs. Date supplies Year and Day. Units_Sold, Revenue, Profit and Stockout are excluded from the predictors.

Random holdout results do not establish future forecasting performance. A chronological evaluation and verified external data are needed before deployment.
